
# Datasets

These datasets are included because they contain direct or closely related measures of human trust, reliance, trust calibration, or human-AI collaboration.

## 1. Anonymized Dataset for "Beyond Trust Increase: Explainability and Accuracy Feedback for Calibrated Human–AI Reliance"

**Source:** Zenodo  
**Year:** 2026  
**DOI:** 10.5281/zenodo.20807611  
**Link:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20807611

**Description:** An anonymized dataset from an online experiment examining feature-based explanations, explicit accuracy feedback, trust change, trust calibration, and intended reliance in AI-assisted decision making.

**Application to this topic:** Useful for studying the relationship between perceived AI accuracy, trust change, calibration gap, and intended reliance.

**License / access:** Check the Zenodo record for the current license and reuse conditions before redistribution.

## 2. Responses from an Adaptive Trust-Calibrated Human–AI Collaboration for Enhanced System Usability

**Source:** Zenodo  
**Year:** 2025/2026 record  
**DOI:** 10.5281/zenodo.18431925  
**Link:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18431925

**Description:** Participant-level interaction data from a controlled human-AI collaboration experiment involving static assistance, autonomous AI, adaptive interfaces, and a trust-calibrated framework.

**Application to this topic:** Directly relevant to measuring trust calibration error, task accuracy, workload, and satisfaction across different human-AI collaboration conditions.

**License / access:** Check the Zenodo record before reuse or redistribution.

## 3. Trust in AI

**Source:** CSIRO Data Access Portal  
**DOI:** 10.25919/cw9k-yz18  
**Link:** https://doi.org/10.25919/cw9k-yz18

**Description:** Research data examining human trust in AI, including studies of trust in AI-generated outputs and comparisons of AI-generated and other machine-generated outputs.

**Application to this topic:** Useful for investigating human trust formation and differences in trust toward AI systems and their outputs.

**License:** The CSIRO record identifies a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence. Verify the current record before reuse.

## Optional Additional Dataset

### Tasks given to Participants of an Adaptive Trust-Calibrated Human–AI Collaboration

**Source:** Zenodo  
**DOI:** 10.5281/zenodo.18432098  
**Link:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18432098

This contains 120 decision-support tasks varying in complexity and simulated AI reliability, with ground-truth labels. It is useful for constructing controlled trust-calibration experiments.

## Dataset Selection Note

Datasets were prioritized for direct relevance to trust calibration, reliance, or human-AI collaboration. The repository does not upload third-party dataset files; it links to the original dataset records so that licensing, provenance, and version information remain attached to the source.
